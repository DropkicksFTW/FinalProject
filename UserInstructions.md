Button of Doom User Guide

Button of Doom is a 3-player game of chance. Players first decide the order they want to play in, then take turns pressing one button at a time.

If a player presses a good button, their turn ends and play moves to the next player. If all 3 players press good buttons, another round begins using the same player order.

If a player presses the bad button, that player is eliminated. The next round then begins with one less active button.
  
The 2 remaining players continue taking turns in the same order until one of them presses the bad button. When this happens, that player is eliminated, and the remaining player wins.

After a winner is chosen, the game resets and starts over with 4 buttons.
